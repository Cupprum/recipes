# Lemon pasta

## Ingredients
* Spaghetti - 250 g
* Organic lemon - 1
* Fresh parsley
* Garlic - 3 medium sized cloves
* Black olives
* Dried tomatoes in oil
* Olive oil
* White wine
* Salt
* Pepper

## Steps
1. Boil water for pasta and add generous amount of salt into it
2. Chop parsley and garlic into tiny bits
3. Grate the lemon zest
4. Heat olive oil in a large pan
5. Add pasta into pot and cook it according to package minus two minutes
6. Add garlic, parsley and lemon zest into the pan
7. Sprinkle with a bit of salt and pepper
8. Add olives and dried tomatoes to the pan
9. Add some water from the pasta into the pan
10. Let the sauce simmer for a bit
11. 3 minutes before the pasta is done add a bit of wine into the pot
12. When the pasta is almost al dente move it to the pan
13. Add a bit more pasta water into the pan
14. Evaporate the excess water for thick sauce
