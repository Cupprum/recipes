# Peanut butter sauce udon noodles

## Ingredients
* Peanut butter - 2 tablespoons
* Soy sauce - 3 tablespoons
* Garlic - 1 medium clove
* Udon noodles
* Water

## Steps
1. Peel the garlic and crush it
2. Add the peanut butter and soy sauce
3. Mix and add water gradually until you achieve the desired consistency and taste, ensuring the sauce is neither too thick nor too thin (add more peanut butter to thicken, more water to thin, and soy sauce to add saltiness)
4. Boil water for the noodles
5. Leave the noodles for 3 minutes in hot water (not boiling)
6. Drain the water and mix the noodles with the sauce
7. Serve immediately after mixing
