# Quesadilla

## Ingredients
* Tortilla
* Tomato sauce with basil
* Bacon
* Cheese - 3 slices (Cheddar or processed cheese slices)
* Rucola (Arugula)
* Oil

## Steps
1. Fry the bacon until it is crisp on the outside and soft on the inside.
2. Spread the tomato sauce and place the cheese slices on the tortilla.
3. Add the fried bacon and rucola onto the tortilla.
4. Fry the assembled tortilla on medium-high heat (setting 6.5) until it is crispy but not burned.
