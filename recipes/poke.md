# Poké

## Ingredients
* Rice
* Water
* Avocado
* Onion
* Black pepper
* Salt
* Chili
* Olive oil
* Mango
* Salad greens
* Tofu
* Soy sauce
* Rice vinegar
* Hoisin sauce
* Sesame seed oil
* Honey
* Carrot

## Steps
1. Combine 3/4 of a cup of rice with 1 and half cups of water and a pinch of salt. Cook for 15 minutes.
2. Prepare the avocado spread by mashing the avocado and mixing it with olive oil, salt, black pepper, and chili.
3. Prepare the marinade for the tofu by mixing 1 part soy sauce, 2 parts hoisin sauce, 1 teaspoon of sesame seed oil, and a small amount of water.
4. Marinate the tofu in the sauce.
5. Fry the tofu in a pan.
6. Toward the end of frying, add 1 tablespoon of honey to the pan to caramelize the tofu.
7. Cut the carrots into thin strips using a vegetable peeler.
8. Cut the mango into cubes.
9. Wash and clean the salad.
10. Place the cooked rice into bowls, cover with the avocado spread, and arrange the tofu, carrot, salad, and mango in quarters on top.
