# Peasant Fondue

## Ingredients
* Whole round bread - 1 loaf
* Sunflower oil
* Sour cream
* Soft processed cheese (triangles/wedges)
* Bell pepper - 1/2
* Leek - 1
* Cubed bacon

## Steps
1. Cut the top off the bread to create a lid.
2. Scoop out the inside of the bread with a spoon, reserving the bread chunks.
3. Place the hollowed bread loaf and the bread chunks on a baking tray.
4. Drizzle a small amount of oil over the bread.
5. Finely chop the bell pepper and the bottom white/light green part of the leek.
6. In a bowl, mix the sour cream, soft cheese triangles, bell pepper, leek, and cubed bacon.
7. Pour the mixture into the hollowed bread loaf.
8. Bake in the oven at 180°C for one hour.
