# Pumpkin risotto

## Ingredients
* Hokkaido pumpkin - 1 medium
* Risotto rice - 500 g
* Onions - 2 medium
* Vegetable bouillon - 1
* Garlic - 3 cloves
* Rosemary
* Thyme
* Black pepper
* Olive oil
* Wine

## Steps
1. Cut the pumpkin into bite-sized chunks, there is no need to peel it.
2. Put the chunks and whole unpeeled garlic cloves on a baking tray.
3. Sprinkle with rosemary, thyme, black pepper, and olive oil.
4. Bake at 200°C until soft.
5. Dissolve the bouillon in a large pot with boiled water.
6. Cut the onions into tiny pieces.
7. Fry the onions with a bit of oil until golden brown in a large pan.
8. Add the risotto rice to the large pan with a scoop of bouillon water.
9. Squish or blend half of the pumpkin chunks and add them to the pan with the risotto.
10. Add a bit of wine to the risotto pan.
11. Stir the risotto frequently and add a scoop of bouillon water from time to time until the rice is ready.
12. Add the rest of the pumpkin chunks to the risotto pan.
