# Crispy Pan-Fried Tofu

## Ingredients
 Tofu
 Cornstarch - 1 tablespoon
 Flour - 1 teaspoon
 Garlic powder - 1 teaspoon
 Salt

## Steps
1. Mix the cornstarch, flour, and garlic powder in a bowl
2. Dry the tofu and cut into pieces
3. Place the tofu into the bowl with the cornstarch mixture and coat thoroughly
4. Transfer the tofu to a medium-hot frying pan and leave unturned for 5 minutes
5. Turn the tofu and fry until it is golden brown
