# Focaccia

## Ingredients
* Pizza dough (600g 00 flour, 360ml water, half a bag of yeast, salt, and sugar - rested for at least 24 hours)
* Rosemary
* Olive oil
* Coarse salt
* Olives
* Dried tomatoes in oil

## Steps
1. Pour a generous amount of olive oil into the baking tray
2. Place the pizza dough into the tray
3. Apply olive oil to the top of the dough
4. Poke the dough with your fingers in several places until you touch the bottom of the tray
5. Sprinkle the rosemary and coarse salt over the dough
6. Cut the olives and dried tomatoes into small pieces
7. Press the olive and tomato pieces into the dough
8. Bake at 200°C using the grill setting for 30 minutes
9. Let the focaccia rest for 10 minutes before cutting
10. Transfer to a wire rack so the bottom does not become soggy
