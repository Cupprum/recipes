# Vegan Goulash

## Ingredients
* Oil
* Onions
* Potatoes
* Paprika
* Black pepper
* Cumin seasoning
* Flour - 1 tablespoon
* Oyster mushrooms (Østers hatte)
* Garlic
* Water
* Bread (for serving)

## Steps
1. Cut the onions and fry them in a pan with the paprika seasoning.
2. Add one tablespoon of flour.
3. Add water into the pan.
4. Peel and cut the potatoes.
5. Cut the mushrooms.
6. Add the potatoes and mushrooms into the pan.
7. Crush the garlic and add it into the pan.
8. If you are not satisfied with the consistency, grate one potato into the pan at the end.
9. Serve with a slice of bread.
