# Overnight oats

## Ingredients
* Milk
* Oats
* Chia seeds
* Peanut butter
* Honey

## Steps
1. Mix one part milk and one part oats for two portions in a bowl
2. Add one tablespoon of chia seeds into the bowl
3. Add a large tablespoon of peanut butter and honey
4. Mix well
5. Leave in the fridge overnight
